01 / THE PURPOSE

Real skills deserve
credible proof.

For Pakistan’s technical and trade workforce, a certificate should open doors — without a trail of paperwork.

THE PAPER PROBLEM

A document isn’t always proof.

Paper certificates can be forged, lost or damaged. Remote checks mean phone calls, manual records and uncertainty for employers.

Easy to alterHard to verify remotely
THE NSP APPROACH

Carry your skills. Prove their origin.

An issuer-signed digital credential belongs to the worker. Its blockchain-anchored fingerprint makes changes detectable and trust independently checkable.

Portable by designCryptographically verifiable
